# Cancellations

## Cooling-off period
A new policy can be cancelled within 31 days of receiving the policy documents, and any premiums already paid are refunded in full, provided no claim has been made.

## Cancelling after the cooling-off period
The policyholder can cancel at any time by giving written notice. Cover ends at the end of the month in which notice is received. Risk-only policies have no cash value, so no money is paid out on cancellation.

## Alternatives to cancelling
Instead of cancelling, a policyholder who is struggling with affordability can reduce the cover amount, remove optional benefits or change the escalation rate to lower the premium.
