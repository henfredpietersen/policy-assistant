# Premiums

## How premiums are paid
Premiums are paid monthly by debit order on the date the policyholder chose when the policy started. The debit order date can be changed by contacting the client care centre.

## Missed premiums and the grace period
If a premium is missed, the policy enters a 30-day grace period during which cover stays in force. If the arrear premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, the policy lapses and cover stops.

## Annual premium increases
Premiums increase every year on the policy anniversary by the escalation rate chosen at the start, for example 5% or 10%. Cover usually increases at the same time so that it keeps up with inflation.

## Reinstating a lapsed policy
A lapsed policy can often be reinstated within 3 months by paying the outstanding premiums. Health questions may need to be answered again before cover is restored.
