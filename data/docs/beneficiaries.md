# Beneficiaries

## Nominating a beneficiary
The policyholder can nominate one or more beneficiaries and choose the percentage of the benefit each one receives. The percentages must add up to 100%.

## Changing beneficiaries
Beneficiaries can be changed at any time while the policy is in force by completing a beneficiary nomination form or updating the details online. The newest valid nomination replaces all earlier nominations.

## Minors as beneficiaries
If a beneficiary is under 18, the benefit is usually paid into a trust or to a legal guardian on behalf of the child, because a minor cannot receive the money directly.

## No beneficiary nominated
If no beneficiary is nominated, or the nominated beneficiary has died, the benefit is paid into the policyholder's estate and distributed according to the will.
