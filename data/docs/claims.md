# Claims

## How to submit a death claim
A beneficiary or family member can submit a death claim by completing the claim form and attaching a certified copy of the death certificate, a certified copy of the claimant's ID and proof of bank details. Claims can be submitted online, by email or at a branch.

## How long a claim takes
Most straightforward claims are paid within 5 working days after all required documents have been received. Claims that need extra medical information or an investigation can take longer, and the claimant is kept updated by SMS and email.

## Disability and dread disease claims
For disability or dread disease claims, the policyholder must submit a claim form, a doctor's report and supporting medical records. The claim is assessed against the policy definitions of disability or the listed illness.

## Why a claim can be declined
A claim can be declined if the policy had lapsed because premiums were not paid, if the event is excluded under the policy terms, or if important information was not disclosed when the policy was taken out.
